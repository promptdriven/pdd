- **Issues:** every *distinct* issue created in-window counts (open **and** closed). Removed before counting: bot-authored issues, Claire's tickets, hackathon/AI-event tickets, literal `test` issues, formal **sub-issues** (children of a parent epic, via the GitHub sub-issue API — counted once through the parent), and **duplicate re-files**. Duplicates are detected by title after normalizing commit-type prefixes, `#`-refs and parenthetical qualifiers; the earliest (lowest-numbered) issue is kept, so a re-file credits the **original filer** — e.g. the 11 `--output extension` re-files credit jamesdlevine (#1148), not the re-filer.
