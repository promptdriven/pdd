# Choose the journeys I check

I can check my product journeys together or choose one by its name.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Checking journeys selects only the journeys in my project’s root journey folder, in their listed order.
2. Choosing J1 selects J1-order and leaves out J10 and nested examples.
3. If J1 names more than one journey, I get a clear request to choose one.
