# Write the prompt and Story

Read the [Prompting guide](../../../docs/prompting-guide.md). Write the behavior in the owning canonical prompt, including important inputs, outcomes, boundaries, and errors. Keep implementation choices out unless they are part of the public contract. Put required prompt dependencies in its `## Dependencies` section.

Write a concise Story with observable acceptance criteria, including what must not happen. Include examples with independent expected outcomes for the important paths. For a visible feature, link the chosen design in the owning prompt and describe its behavior and accessibility in words. Mark drafts as unapproved until the person reviews them.
